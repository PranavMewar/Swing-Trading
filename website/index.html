The framework

The right mindset
produces the right trader.

Markets do not reward the smartest. They reward the most disciplined. Every trader we mentor is built in the same order — psychology first, setups next, execution last. Skip a step, and the next one collapses.

  1. i

    01 — PSYCHOLOGY

    The foundation.

    Rewire fear, FOMO, and impulse before they ever cost you a rupee. Build the temperament that survives bull runs, bear traps, and every market in between. Without this, no setup will save you.

  2. ii

    02 — SETUPS

    The framework.

    Learn the high-revenue swing patterns that repeat across cycles, sectors, and stocks. A weekly watchlist co-built live on Google Meet — so you see how a professional thinks, not just what they pick.

  3. iii

    03 — EXECUTION

    Flawless, with confidence.

    Pull the trigger without hesitation, sized correctly, and exit with conviction — not emotion. Daily trade reviews close the feedback loop, so every trade either earns you money or earns you a lesson.

The programme

Everything you receive,
across every tier.

  • Psychology Rewiring — the core

    A structured curriculum that retrains how you think before, during, and after a trade. The single biggest variable separating profitable and losing traders.

  • High-Revenue Setups — pattern library

    A working library of the swing setups that consistently produce — taught with entries, stops, targets, and the conditions in which each one works (and fails).

  • Weekly Watchlist on Google Meet — live, with members

    Built live with the cohort every week. You see the screening, the rejection logic, and the final shortlist take shape in real time.

    Bring your charts, your trades, your blind spots. Two open sessions a month for deep dialogue and tactical answers.

  • Daily Market Report — Telegram broadcast

    A concise read on market posture every trading day, broadcast straight to your Telegram. Context before the bell rings.

  • Daily Trade Reports & Feedback — the feedback loop

    Submit your trade reports daily and receive specific feedback on what was right, what was wrong, and what to repeat or rebuild. This is where compounding actually starts.

A complete loop — learn, execute, review, refine.

Programme trajectory

A deliberate sequence —
not a scattered curriculum.

The first three months focus only and only on psychology, risk management, and position sizing. No shortcuts. The following months scale according to the demand of the market — building setups, sharpening execution, and tightening the feedback loop.

M 01 – 03

Foundations

Psychology rewiring. Risk management. Position sizing. The non-negotiable groundwork that decides whether everything above it stands or falls.

M 04 – 06

Pattern fluency

The high-revenue setup library, taught with conditions of strength and conditions of failure. Weekly watchlists built live with the cohort.

M 07 – 09

Live execution

Daily trade reports, daily feedback. Bi-weekly Q&A. Sizing and sequencing tightened against real, dated outcomes — yours.

M 10 – 12

Personal playbook

A written, audited record of your edge — the setups, conditions, sizing, and review protocol you'll carry beyond the programme.
